# The 3-Minute Script (≈445 words · rehearse to 2:45 — the cutoff is HARD, timer on screen)

> Matches the 10-slide plan in [`pitch-plan.md`](./pitch-plan.md) §3. Numbers live on the slides;
> the voice carries the story. Words in [brackets] are choices to make. Slow down on the **bold**
> beats. If rehearsal runs past 2:50, cut from slides 2–3, never from 4, 5 or 9.
> Language: the brief prefers **English** (some jury members are English-speaking); Arabic is
> accepted where you're more comfortable — if you mix, keep slides in English and deliver the
> story beats (0:20, 1:30) in whichever language carries your conviction best. The brief says
> judges remember conviction as clearly as numbers.

**[0:00 · Slide 1 — Title + Vision & Mission]**
We're Nasam — the operating layer for Saudi commerce. We take brands live on every sales channel
and run them — from onboarding to cashflow. Our mission: one platform, every channel, for every
Saudi brand. Our vision: when a Saudi brand sells anywhere — it runs through Nasam.

**[0:20 · Slide 2 — The Problem]**
We didn't find this problem in a report — we lived it, running twenty-five brands for ten
companies. Every morning: five dashboards, fifty checks. A coffee brand winning on Salla,
invisible on Amazon — same products, different rules. Saudi shoppers moved to the marketplaces —
they convert **three times better** than a brand's own store. Eighty thousand merchants never made
the jump. The demand moved. The brands didn't.

**[0:50 · Slide 3 — Our Solution]**
So we built the bridge ourselves. One screen replaces five dashboards — products, orders,
inventory, profit — across Amazon, noon, Trendyol, Salla, Jahez — with a team that acts on it.
Two things nobody else has: Brand Health, ten dimensions graded daily — and AI access to your live
commerce data.

**[1:10 · Slide 4 — Business Model]**
The model in one sentence: **brands pay us monthly for the platform — and as they grow, they hand
us more of the operation, until we run everything and earn a percentage of every riyal they
sell.** Recurring — and it compounds with our brands' growth.

**[1:30 · Slide 5 — Revenue & Growth]**
Is it working? The past twelve months: two million riyals of GMV — and the last ninety days did
four times the nine months before them. One brand — Sonbol: **four hundred ninety-five riyals in
June; eight hundred twenty-two thousand in nineteen days of August.** Our three-year projection:
assumptions attached, no hockey stick.

**[1:55 · Slide 6 — Market & Competition]**
The market: twenty-five billion dollars of Saudi e-commerce, growing twelve percent a year, forty
thousand registered online businesses. Our rivals are real — agencies, ChannelEngine, Pattern —
and the biggest: doing nothing. Nobody else is software AND operations, every channel.

**[2:10 · Slide 7 — Founders & Team]**
Three founders, one closed loop: Fahad builds it, Ibrahim brings brands in, Tarik makes them
succeed. Letting go of our smallest brands taught us where we win: brands ready to scale.

**[2:20 · Slide 8 — What Makes Us Special]**
Are we the next Uber? Uber didn't own cars. We don't own brands — **we run the rails every Saudi
brand rides to every marketplace.** And you saw the rails work — Sonbol.

**[2:35 · Slide 9 — stc × Nasam]**
So why InspireU — specifically? One: our merchants are your customers — bundle Nasam through
solutions by stc: the Gulf's first telco commerce offer. Two: stc Bank is rolling out merchant
lending; we hold what it can't see — verified marketplace sales data. **stc Bank IS the
cashflow.** Three: you backed Rewaa for retail and Zid for webstores — Nasam is the marketplaces.

**[2:55 · Slide 10 — Close]**
This year: [target]. In five years: the commerce infrastructure of the Gulf — with stc. Thank you.
