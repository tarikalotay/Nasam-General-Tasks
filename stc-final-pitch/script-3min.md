**[0:00 · S1 — Vision & Mission]**
We're Nasam — the operating layer for commerce. We take brands live on every sales channel and run
them, from onboarding to cashflow. Our vision: when a brand sells anywhere — it runs through Nasam.

**[0:20 · S2 — The problem]**
Seventy-eight thousand brands sell from their own storefronts. Fewer than twenty thousand ever
reach the marketplaces, where conversion is three times higher. Why? The operations wall:
listings, inventory, fulfillment, pricing, ads — per channel, every day. And it costs them time,
money, and quality: fifty checks a day, penalties and lost Buy Box, listings that slip.
Fifty-eight thousand brands stay stuck behind it.

**[0:45 · S3 — Our solution]**
Nasam opens the bottleneck. One portal with every signal a brand needs — orders, inventory,
pricing, profit — across every channel, with Brand Health graded daily and AI on live data. And
when a brand wants it run for them, our team runs it on the same platform. Why now? The demand
is already paid for, the shelf is uncaptured, and the season won't wait.

**[1:10 · S4 — Business model]**
One platform, three ways to run it. Software: four-forty-nine a month, or three-nine-nine-nine a
year. Onboarding — a one-time payment, platform included, we take you live. Full management — a
monthly fee plus a share of GMV, everything included, we are their commerce team.

**[1:35 · S5 — Revenue & growth]**
The last twelve months of revenue: from fourteen hundred riyals a month to thirty-one and a half
thousand — a quarter-million riyals total. Two million of GMV through the platform,
twenty-five-plus brands across ten accounts, fifteen thousand orders.

A twenty-five-billion-dollar market — and only one player in the region runs every channel.
Agencies do one channel. ChannelEngine is software only. Pattern buys your inventory. We're the
only software with a management team, across every local marketplace — Arabic-first.
