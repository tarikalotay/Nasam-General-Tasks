## 4. The problem as an emotional story (idea → today → future)

Told in the founders' voice — this is the arc the user asked for, and it is TRUE to the data:

- **Act 1 — we lived it (the idea).** "We weren't consultants watching this problem. We were the
  operators — running 25+ brands for 10 companies across Saudi marketplaces. Every morning: five
  dashboards, fourteen graded signals, fifty checks. A coffee brand we ran was winning on Salla and
  invisible on Amazon — same products, same country, different rules." *(true: Nokush/Thannah are
  coffee brands in the portfolio — [EDIT ME: pick the real anecdote you can defend])*
- **Act 2 — the market moved, brands didn't.** "Saudi shoppers moved to the marketplaces — they
  convert at 9.5% versus 2–3% on a brand's own store. But 78,000 Saudi merchants still sell only
  from their own storefront, and fewer than 20,000 ever made the jump. Not because they don't want
  the demand — because six decisions stand before the first sale, and fifty checks stand between
  them and every day after it."
- **Act 3 — so we built the bridge (today).** → transitions into the solution slide.
- **Act 4 — where it goes (the future).** "Every brand that makes the jump grows the whole
  ecosystem — that's the future we're building, and with stc it comes years sooner." → sets up
  slides 10–11.

Why this works for pillar 02's card: the pain is **real** (we lived it), **current** (the 78K/20K
gap is today), **expensive** (a 3–4× conversion difference is money left on the table daily; 1.6%
of sellers capture half the GMV).

## 5. Business model — the anchor slide (per team: the judges' #1 decision factor)

**The one-sentence model (a judge must be able to repeat it):**

> "Brands pay us monthly for the platform — and as they grow, they hand us more of the operation,
> until we run everything and earn a percentage of every riyal they sell."

Card answers, explicit:
- **Who pays:** the brand (SME → mid-market → enterprise).
- **For what:** platform subscription → onboarding → full account management.
- **At what price:** SAR 449/month platform (~SAR 4,700/yr [EDIT ME: deck shows "449 × 1.5 SAR/month" — settle the real list price before the pitch]) · one-time onboarding fee · management retainer + % of operated GMV.
- **How often:** monthly, recurring — with the % of GMV growing as the brand grows.

**Why it can grow and scale (say this out loud):**
1. **Land-and-expand is built into the funnel** — customers move *down* it as they grow; ARPU rises
   without new acquisition cost.
2. **Revenue is GMV-linked** — Nasam's income compounds with its brands' growth (Sonbol is the live
   proof: platform GMV 40× in 8 months with the same core team).
3. **The services teach the software** — every operational task we do manually becomes a platform
   feature; margin structurally improves over time.
4. **Unlockable streams later (Q5 on the card):** marketplace-ads management (% of ad spend), seller
   financing referrals on live sales data, benchmark data products, AI-agent access seats.
