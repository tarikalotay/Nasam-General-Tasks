The business-model sentence

The team's call: this is the judges' #1 decision factor. If a judge cannot repeat it back, it isn't clear enough yet.

"Brands pay us monthly for the platform — and as they grow, they hand us more of the operation, until we run everything and earn a percentage of every riyal they sell."
Why it scales: land-and-expand is built into the funnel · revenue is GMV-linked (Sonbol is the live proof) · every manual service becomes a platform feature · unlockable later: ads %, seller financing, data, AI seats.

The centerpiece

stc × Nasam — the two answers

InspireU's stated mandate is "creating value for stc by linking startups into the stc ecosystem." This slide answers the exam question in their own language.

What Nasam adds to stc Group

  1. A new SME revenue line. Vodafone runs 13 telco SaaS marketplaces; e& bundles commerce tools. Through solutions by stc, stc becomes the first GCC telco with a marketplace-commerce offer — DARE's "Expand" pillar.
  2. Data stc Bank can't see. 200K+ registered merchants, SME lending rolling out — and no view of marketplace sales. Nasam holds the verified cross-channel revenue data that underwrites merchant working capital.
  3. The missing portfolio piece. stc backed Rewaa (offline retail SaaS) and Zid (webstores, InspireU alum). Nasam is the third layer: marketplaces. Together — the full Saudi retail stack.

What stc adds to Nasam

  1. Distribution we can't buy. A co-sell pilot through solutions by stc / stc Business SME customers and stc Bank's merchant base — the first [500] merchants in 2027.
  2. The fintech rails. stc Bank partnership: merchant payouts + the Kingdom's first marketplace-data-underwritten working-capital product, piloted on our live brands. (The 11th batch's fintech track produced real commercial agreements — we're asking for that path.)
  3. Capital + credibility, on a named path. SAR 100K equity-free grant + ~SAR 600K services now; tali ventures follow-on up to $500K as we scale; the stc name behind us with enterprise brands.

Held for Q&A: sirar security certification · stc cloud / center3 data residency (PDPL) · iot squared logistics telemetry. And if a food-delivery judge is in the room (Jahez's CEO judged the Bahrain final): "we run brands on Jahez and HungerStation alongside Amazon and noon — nobody else does."

The full script — word for word (~445 words · rehearse to 2:45)

0:00 · Title + Vision & Mission

We're Nasam — the operating layer for Saudi commerce. We take brands live on every sales channel and run them — from onboarding to cashflow. Our mission: one platform, every channel, for every Saudi brand. Our vision: when a Saudi brand sells anywhere — it runs through Nasam.

0:20 · The Problem

We didn't find this problem in a report — we lived it, running twenty-five brands for ten companies. Every morning: five dashboards, fifty checks. A coffee brand winning on Salla, invisible on Amazon — same products, different rules. Saudi shoppers moved to the marketplaces — they convert three times better than a brand's own store. Eighty thousand merchants never made the jump. The demand moved. The brands didn't.

0:50 · Our Solution

So we built the bridge ourselves. One screen replaces five dashboards — products, orders, inventory, profit — across Amazon, noon, Trendyol, Salla, Jahez — with a team that acts on it. Two things nobody else has: Brand Health, ten dimensions graded daily — and AI access to your live commerce data.

1:10 · Business Model

The model in one sentence: brands pay us monthly for the platform — and as they grow, they hand us more of the operation, until we run everything and earn a percentage of every riyal they sell. Recurring — and it compounds with our brands' growth.

1:30 · Revenue & Growth

Is it working? The past twelve months: two million riyals of GMV — and the last ninety days did four times the nine months before them. One brand — Sonbol: four hundred ninety-five riyals in June; eight hundred twenty-two thousand in nineteen days of August. Our three-year projection: assumptions attached, no hockey stick.

1:55 · Market & Competition

The market: twenty-five billion dollars of Saudi e-commerce, growing twelve percent a year, forty thousand registered online businesses. Our rivals are real — agencies, ChannelEngine, Pattern — and the biggest: doing nothing. Nobody else is software AND operations, every channel.

2:10 · Founders & Team

Three founders, one closed loop: Fahad builds it, Ibrahim brings brands in, Tarik makes them succeed. Letting go of our smallest brands taught us where we win: brands ready to scale.

2:20 · What Makes Us Special

Are we the next Uber? Uber didn't own cars. We don't own brands — we run the rails every Saudi brand rides to every marketplace. And you saw the rails work — Sonbol.

2:35 · stc × Nasam

So why InspireU — specifically? One: our merchants are your customers — bundle Nasam through solutions by stc: the Gulf's first telco commerce offer. Two: stc Bank is rolling out merchant lending; we hold what it can't see — verified marketplace sales data. stc Bank IS the cashflow. Three: you backed Rewaa for retail and Zid for webstores — Nasam is the marketplaces.

2:55 · Close

This year: [target]. In five years: the commerce infrastructure of the Gulf — with stc. Thank you.
