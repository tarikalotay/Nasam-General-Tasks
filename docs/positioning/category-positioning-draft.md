# Nasam category positioning — working draft

Status: draft for internal discussion (Tarik), 4 Sep 2026. Not shared with the group yet.
Source of truth for facts: ClickUp → Nasam Strategic Documents → 📘 2026 Strategy (3 Sep 2026).

## 1. The real problem

The WhatsApp framing says the market has no category for us. The strategy documents show the
opposite: the category exists and is well known (Rithum/ChannelAdvisor, ChannelEngine, Linnworks
globally; Linker locally). What is missing is *one* sentence Nasam uses everywhere. Today four
stories are live at the same time:

| Where | What it says Nasam is |
| --- | --- |
| V1 2026 strategy (Jan) | Managed service, explicitly "not self-serve SaaS" |
| 📘 2026 Strategy (Sep) | SaaS first; management is the upgrade |
| NTDP company profile | "AI-driven account management agency" |
| Pitch, section 5 "next position" | Infrastructure other operators (agencies, 3PLs) need |

The fix is internal alignment, not category creation.

## 2. Answer to "infrastructure for what?"

Sales channels. Oto connects a store to every carrier; Omniful runs the warehouse; Nasam connects
a store to every marketplace and runs the selling. The strategy already says this
("the role Omniful plays in warehousing, applied to channels").

Note on the analogy in the message: Oto and Torod are shipping aggregators, not OMS. Use
"Oto for shipping, Nasam for marketplaces" — it is cleaner and every Salla merchant knows Oto.

## 3. The six acronyms, scored

| Candidate | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MMS — Marketplace Management Software | Keep the phrase, drop the acronym | Matches the global category name and Fahad's existing bio ("Marketplace Management Platform"). MMS as an acronym means multimedia messaging. |
| MCIS — Multi-Channel Integration Software | No | Describes the plumbing only. That is ChannelEngine's and Linker's corner, and the strategy says "sell seats, do not license the connectors". |
| MCE — Multi-Channel E-commerce Enablement | No | "Enablement" is consultant language; no buyer searches for it. |
| MMSO — Managed Marketplace Operations | No as the umbrella | Describes package 3 only and contradicts SaaS-first. Fine as the *name of package 3*. |
| MCA — Multi-Channel Aggregator | No | "Aggregator" reads as Pattern/Opontia (they buy the brand) or a reseller. |
| MCS — Multi-Channel Software | No | Too generic to mean anything. |

Do not invent an acronym. Category education costs years and budget; Nasam has 8 brands and
~21K/month. Anchor to a category the buyer already understands and add one differentiator.

## 4. Proposed positioning (two audiences, one product)

**Category (the shelf):** Marketplace Management Platform
Arabic: منصة إدارة قنوات البيع / منصة إدارة الأسواق الإلكترونية

**Differentiator (why us on that shelf):** the only one in the region with a management team on
top, and built local: Arabic and English stores, every Saudi marketplace, Amazon Retail POs,
quick commerce, Brand Health graded daily.

**Positioning statement**
For brands on Salla, Zid or Shopify with 20+ orders a month that want to sell on Amazon, Noon,
Trendyol and beyond, Nasam is the marketplace management platform that runs every channel from
one place. Unlike Linker or ChannelEngine, which only connect, Nasam comes with the team that
manages it when you want them to.

**Merchant one-liner (Arabic):**
نسم تربط متجرك بكل سوق إلكتروني وتدير طلباتك ومخزونك وأسعارك وربحيتك من مكان واحد، ومعها فريق يديرها عنك لو تبغى. أوتو للشحن، نسم للأسواق.

**Investor / partner / NTDP line:**
Nasam is the marketplace infrastructure layer for the region: the same platform that runs our
managed brands is sold as seats to agencies, 3PLs and consultancies that cannot build seven
regional integrations themselves.

Rule: merchants hear "platform"; partners and investors hear "infrastructure". Same product,
same category, different word for the audience.

## 5. How it maps to the three packages

| Package | Public name | One line |

| 1 Platform (449/mo) | المنصة | You run it. Every channel, one place. |

The ladder is the story: same platform at every step, more of Nasam's people as you go up.

## 6. Wording to fix in the WhatsApp draft

- "B2B and B2C": both are B2B (the client is always a brand). What is meant is self-serve vs
  managed, and marketplace vs retail (Amazon Retail POs). Say that instead.
- "Not repeated": say "no one in the region combines software and a management team" rather than
  "the idea has no precedent". The precedent is what makes it explainable.
- "If we start marketing tomorrow": mass marketing is gated on W3, W4, W5 and opens in November.
  Positioning is not gated and should be settled now so November launches with one message.
