# Phase II — TLD Measurement and Procedural Data Protocol

## Purpose

Provide objective, individual-level occupational radiation exposure measurement under routine working conditions, before and after the educational course, so that questionnaire-based findings can be linked to measurable exposure.

## Participants

- Subgroup of healthcare professionals who routinely participate in cardiac catheterization procedures.
- Selected using an appropriate sampling method (e.g., stratified random sampling by professional role) to reduce selection bias.
- Sample is **signal-generating / hypothesis-generating** — not intended as a national estimate.

## Measurement Schedule

| Period | Duration | Instrument | Concurrent data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | ~1 month of routine clinical work | TLD #1 | Baseline KAP questionnaire; workload/procedural data; objective practice observation where feasible |
| Educational course | 1 day | — | Course attendance recorded |
| Post-course | ~1 month of routine clinical work | TLD #2 (replaces TLD #1) | Post-course KAP questionnaire; same workload/procedural data; objective practice observation where feasible |

## Open Protocol Decisions (to finalize with medical physics — roadmap step 9)

- **Badge placement:** number of TLDs per participant and position (e.g., collar outside apron, under-apron chest) and the dose-estimation convention to be used — per institutional dosimetry service and national regulatory practice.
- **Dosimetry service:** processing laboratory, calibration, minimum reportable dose, and control badges (background subtraction) per center.
- **Handling rules:** badges worn for all radiation-based procedures and only by the assigned participant; storage away from radiation sources when not worn; loss/damage reporting procedure.
- **Comparability:** same badge type, placement, wear rules, and processing laboratory across both measurement periods.

## Procedural / Workload Data (where feasible, both periods)

- Number of procedures attended
- Procedure type and procedural complexity
- Fluoroscopy time
- Dose–area product (DAP/KAP)
- Air kerma
- Other available radiation-dose parameters
- Participant's typical role/position during procedures

These variables allow the pre–post exposure comparison to account for differences in workload and procedural characteristics:

```
Baseline month:    TLD exposure + workload + practice
        versus
Post-course month: TLD exposure + workload + practice
```

## Blinding / Hawthorne-Effect Mitigation

- Participants are **not** informed of the specific pre–post comparison (that exposure and behavior before vs. after the course are being compared to determine whether the course reduces exposure).
- Informed consent and all research-ethics requirements are maintained; any concealment of the specific hypothesis is implemented only to the extent permitted by the research ethics committee.
- Preferred design label: **prospective educational study with partially blinded or undisclosed outcome assessment** (not "double-blind").

## Ethics and Safety Notes

- Ethics/IRB approval required before any data collection (roadmap step 12).
- Study TLDs complement — never replace — any institutionally mandated dosimetry.
- If any study measurement suggests exposure of clinical/regulatory concern, the participant and the institutional radiation safety officer are informed per local policy; participant safety overrides study blinding.
- Data are stored under anonymized participant codes linking questionnaire, TLD, and procedural data.
