# Radiation Safety Competency Index (RSCI) — Development and Validation Framework

The RSCI is the potentially novel contribution of this study: a composite measure of a practitioner's overall readiness to work safely in a radiation-based clinical environment.

## Conceptual Model

```
Knowledge + Attitude + Practice + Training/Education
                      ↓
        Radiation Safety Competency (RSCI, 0–100)
```

Objective workplace observations may also contribute to the assessment where feasible.

> **Core principle:** knowledge does not necessarily equal competency. Knowledge → understanding; competency → knowledge translated into safe clinical practice.

## Provisional Scoring Structure

⚠ **Provisional only — final weights, scoring, and thresholds must come from psychometric validation, not be fixed in advance.**

| Domain | Source (questionnaire section) | Provisional weight |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Section B (20 items; correct = 1, incorrect or "I don't know" = 0) | 40% |
| Practice | Section D (12 items, 1–5 frequency scale), plus objective observation where available | 30% |
| Attitude | Section C (13 items, 1–5 Likert; C11 reverse-considered at validation) | 15% |
| Training | Section E (recency, duration, frequency, formal assessment) | 15% |

Each domain is normalized to 0–100 before weighting, yielding a composite RSCI of 0–100.

### Provisional Competency Classification

⚠ Categories and thresholds to be determined after validation (e.g., via standard-setting methods or distribution-based cut points):

- Inadequate competency
- Developing competency
- Adequate competency
- High competency

## Validation Pipeline

| Step | Method | Target / criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Content validity | Expert panel (interventional cardiologist, medical physicist, radiation safety officer, nursing/technologist representative); item-level and scale-level Content Validity Index | I-CVI ≥ 0.78; S-CVI/Ave ≥ 0.90 (typical thresholds; confirm with study statistician) |
| 2. Face validity & pilot | Pilot on a small sample (~20–30) outside the main study centers; comprehension, timing, ceiling/floor effects | Revise ambiguous items |
| 3. Reliability | Internal consistency per domain (Cronbach's α / McDonald's ω); test–retest on pilot subsample where feasible (ICC) | α ≥ 0.70 per domain |
| 4. Item analysis | Difficulty and discrimination indices for knowledge items; item–total correlations for Likert scales | Flag items for revision/removal |
| 5. Construct validity | Exploratory factor analysis (and confirmatory analysis if sample allows) to test the four-domain structure; inter-domain correlations | Model fit and interpretability |
| 6. Known-groups validity | Compare RSCI across groups expected to differ (e.g., recent formal training vs. none; roles) | Significant expected differences |
| 7. Criterion-related evidence | Relationship between RSCI (especially practice domain) and objective measures: observed practice, workload-adjusted TLD exposure | Exploratory in this study |
| 8. Scoring & thresholds | Finalize weights (e.g., informed by factor loadings/regression on objective outcomes) and competency cut points | Documented, reproducible scoring rule |

## Analysis Notes (to feed the statistical analysis plan)

- **Phase I primary outcome:** overall RSCI (and domain scores) across centers and professional roles; predictors of competency via regression (role, experience, training recency, institutional program availability).
- **Phase II educational outcome:** pre–post change in knowledge, attitude, practice scores (paired tests; effect sizes).
- **Phase II occupational outcome:** pre–post change in TLD-measured exposure, adjusted for workload (procedure count, type, complexity, fluoroscopy time, DAP/KAP) — e.g., exposure normalized per procedure or per unit DAP, or workload-adjusted models.
- Self-reported vs. observed practice analyzed and reported separately; discordance itself is an outcome of interest.
- "I don't know" responses analyzed both as incorrect (scoring) and descriptively (distinguishing absent knowledge from misinformation — wrong answer vs. "I don't know").
- Sample-size calculation (roadmap step 8) driven by the Phase II paired pre–post comparisons and Phase I precision requirements.
