# Radiation Safety Competency Study

**Radiation Safety Competency and Occupational Radiation Exposure among Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory Personnel — A Multicenter KAP Assessment and Prospective Educational Study**

This repository contains the research protocol, study instruments, and supporting documents for a two-phase study assessing radiation safety competency among healthcare professionals working in cardiac catheterization laboratories in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

## Central Research Question

> Are healthcare professionals working in radiation-based cardiac specialties in Saudi Arabia adequately competent to practice safely with ionizing radiation, and can a focused radiation-safety educational course improve their knowledge, attitudes, and practices and translate these improvements into measurable reductions in occupational radiation exposure?

## Study at a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Phase I** | Multicenter KAP and Radiation Safety Competency assessment (6 cardiac centers, Riyadh) |
| **Phase II** | Prospective educational study with TLD-measured occupational exposure before and after a focused one-day course |
| **Novel contribution** | Development and validation of the **Radiation Safety Competency Index (RSCI)** |
| **Design note** | Prospective educational study with partially blinded / undisclosed outcome assessment (Hawthorne-effect mitigation, subject to ethics committee approval) |

## Guiding Principle

> **Radiation safety should be treated as a clinical competency, not merely as a body of knowledge.**

From radiation-safety awareness to measurable competency, from competency to safer practice, and from safer practice to reduced occupational radiation exposure.
